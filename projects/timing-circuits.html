how-it-works/

01

A bucket keeps time

The capacitor is a bucket and the resistor is a narrow pipe. Fill speed is the beat.

02

Meet the 555

One cheap chip fills the bucket, drains it, and repeats. That is a blinking light.

03

Two gates that remember

SET and RESET flip a loop of gates. Let go and it still remembers.

04

Try it yourself

Hold S or R on the keyboard, swap NOR and NAND, change the bucket and pipe.

No build step. The whole deck is one HTML file with hand-drawn SVG.

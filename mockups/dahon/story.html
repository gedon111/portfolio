Our story

It started with one leaf and a moka pot.

Lola Remy kept a pandan plant by the kitchen window and dropped a knotted leaf into every pot of rice. Years later her granddaughter tied one around a moka pot handle during a brownout, and the whole apartment smelled like home. That smell became the cafe.

Dahon opened in 2022 with four stools and one grinder. We buy green beans straight from farms in Atok and Sagada, roast them in small batches every Monday, and bake with the same pandan, ube and muscovado we grew up with.

We are still small. The long table seats twelve, the window seats are first come first served, and Kape the cat decides who gets the armchair.

“Make it the way Lola would, just a little slower.”House rule no. 1

  1. 2019

    Pop-up stall at the Saturday night market. Pandan latte sells out by 8.

  2. 2021

    First direct trade with a family farm in Sagada.

  3. 2022

    The cafe opens above the bookshop on Session Road.

  4. 2024

    A small roaster arrives. Mondays become roast days.

Car rental with the route included

Pick up at seven. Be on the ridge by nine.

Ten car classes at four branches. Each one comes with a road trip we've already driven. Kilometres are unlimited, and you can return the car to any branch.

Ten cars, washed and fuelled before every pickup

Prices are per day and include insurance, unlimited kilometres and a second driver. The model you get is the one shown or an equivalent from the same class.

Three routes, drawn for the car you take

Each route starts and ends at a branch. Choose one to see it on the map. You'll get the turn-by-turn file with your booking.

Keys in hand in under ten minutes

01

Book online, pay at pickup

Hold a car for free. You can cancel up to 24 hours before pickup, and nothing is charged until you sign.

02

Collect at a branch

Show your licence and card at the desk. The car is parked out front with a full tank or charge.

03

Return it to any branch

Drop it at whichever branch is nearest the end of your trip. No one-way fee between our four branches.

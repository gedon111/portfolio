About the studio

Started in a carport, now a four-bay studio.

Kintab means shine. We began washing cars for neighbours on weekends and grew into an indoor studio with filtered water and proper lighting, so we can see every swirl.

How we work

The same four steps, every car.

  1. Inspect

    We walk around the car with you under bright lights and note every scratch, so there are no surprises.

  2. Decontaminate

    Foam soak, two-bucket wash, iron remover and clay. The paint has to be truly clean before any polish.

  3. Correct and protect

    Machine polish where needed, then sealant, coating or film depending on your package.

  4. Hand over

    A final check together, care tips for the rainy season, and photos of the work.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a ceramic coating last here in the Philippines?

Ours is rated for three years. Strong sun and acid rain shorten any coating, so we include a free one-month check and recommend a maintenance wash every two to four weeks.

Can I wait while you work?

For washes and interiors, yes. Our lounge has seats, Wi-Fi and coffee. Paint correction and coatings take one to four days, so we'll text when it's ready.

Will polishing remove deep scratches?

If your fingernail catches on it, it's likely through the clear coat and needs touch-up paint. We'll tell you honestly before we start.

Do you do home service?

Washes only, within Quezon City, for a ₱300 travel fee. Correction and coatings need our controlled lighting and filtered water.
